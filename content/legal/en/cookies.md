## What are cookies?
Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) are small files the website stores on your device.

## Cookies used on this website
| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| cookie-consent (localStorage) | Technical, first-party | Remember your cookie choice | Until you clear it |
| quote-list (localStorage) | Technical, first-party | Store your quote list | Until you clear it |
| lang (localStorage) | Technical, first-party | Remember your language | Until you clear it |
| [ANALYTICS – only if enabled] | Analytics, third-party | Anonymous usage statistics | [DURATION] |

Technical cookies are necessary and do not require consent. Analytics cookies only load if you accept them in the banner.

## Managing cookies
You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser; the banner will reappear. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

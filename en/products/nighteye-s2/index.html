Overview

The NightEye S2 features a large-format Sony STARVIS 2 sensor (1/1.8") that captures detail and colour in very low light, from dawn flights to night-time inspection and surveillance missions.

Its 160° wide-angle view and modes of up to 100 fps deliver a smooth, expansive picture from a body measuring just 19 × 19 × 24 mm and weighing 9.5 g.

Extreme sensitivity0.0001 lux minimum illumination.

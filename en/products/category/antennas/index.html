5.8 GHz circularly polarized antennas for a clean, stable link.

Drone accessories that protect the video link: compact 5.8 GHz left-hand circularly polarized (LHCP) omnidirectional antennas. The OmniWave 5.8 LHCP covers 5600–6000 MHz with 1.9 dBi average gain and VSWR ≤ 1.5, weighs 2 g per unit and uses an IPEX-1 connector for clean integration on OEM airframes.

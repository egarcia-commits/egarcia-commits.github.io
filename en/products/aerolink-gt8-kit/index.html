Overview

The AeroLink GT8 Kit puts everything you need for high-definition digital video on a drone into a single box: an actively cooled transmitter, a low-light camera with a Sony STARVIS 2 sensor and a pair of circularly polarized antennas.

Built for integrators and OEM platforms, it talks to the flight controller over UART to display telemetry on screen, and records every flight to a micro SD card right in the air.

Key features

Low latency22 ms average for precise control.
Night visionSTARVIS 2 sensor, 0.0001 lux minimum illumination.
Onboard recording1080p/720p to micro SD up to 256 GB.
Active coolingFan built into the transmitter.
Full integrationOSD with Betaflight, INAV, FETtec, KISS and ArduPilot.
8 channelsFly several drones at once on 5.8 GHz.

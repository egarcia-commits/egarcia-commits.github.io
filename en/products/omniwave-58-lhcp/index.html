Overview

The OmniWave 5.8 LHCP are compact left-hand circularly polarized antennas that reduce signal reflections and keep the link stable when flying around obstacles.

A direct replacement for the AeroLink GT8 transmitter, with IPEX-1 connector and a Ø 15 × 45 mm cylindrical protective housing.

Key features

5600–6000 MHzCovers the full 5.8 GHz band.
LHCP polarizationLess multipath interference.
50 Ω · VSWR ≤1.5Efficient power transfer.
Ultralight2 g per unit.

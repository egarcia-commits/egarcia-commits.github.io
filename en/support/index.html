Support & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Are your video systems compatible with my goggles?

Our GT8 system works with goggles and receivers from the compatible HD digital ecosystem. Send us your ground equipment model and we'll confirm compatibility before you order.

Which flight controllers are supported for OSD?

The AeroLink GT8 VTX supports Canvas-mode OSD (MSP DisplayPort) with Betaflight, INAV, FETtec, KISS and ArduPilot.

Can I use the ThermaView 960 with the GT8 or PX transmitter?

Yes. The ThermaView 960 is available in two versions: the GT8 version requires the AeroLink GT8 VTX (ground unit in race mode) and the PX version requires the AeroLink PX transmitter.

Do you offer volume or distributor pricing?

Yes. Use the Distributors page or the quote list to tell us the products and quantities you need and we'll send you a tailored offer.

Can I sell your products under my own brand?

White-label and private-label options are available for distributors and OEMs, subject to minimum quantities. Contact our sales team.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your quote list and send the request by email or WhatsApp. We confirm price, availability and delivery time, then send you a pro-forma invoice. Online checkout is coming soon.

What warranty do products have?

Consumer purchases in the EU are covered by the legal guarantee of conformity. Business (B2B) warranty terms are stated in each offer. See our Terms & Conditions.

Am I allowed to transmit at full power?

Transmit power limits depend on your country's radio regulations (e.g. CE <14 dBm EIRP for 5.8 GHz in the EU). Users are responsible for configuring and operating equipment in compliance with local law.

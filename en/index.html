11 November 2026European Commission – DG MOVE

EU Drone Summit sets the course for a secure European drone market and a Trusted Drone Label

More than 400 industry and government participants met in Brussels to review the EU Drone Strategy 2.0. The first findings of the European Trusted Drone Label pilot were published, and an implementation roadmap is due in 2027.

#regulation#europe

9 November 2026Aerospace America (AIAA)

DARPA EVADE: flight tests continue for Sikorsky's Nomad long-endurance VTOL drone

Sikorsky's flying-wing Nomad prototype is one of the designs flying under DARPA's $47M EVADE programme, which seeks uncrewed VTOL aircraft with fixed-wing endurance.

#industry#research#vtol

9 November 2026Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)

FAA grants first-of-its-kind authorization for long-range drone flights from the US coast to offshore platforms

The US regulator authorized an autonomous VTOL drone to fly beyond visual line of sight from Louisiana to an offshore energy platform, including a pipeline inspection and a simulated equipment delivery.

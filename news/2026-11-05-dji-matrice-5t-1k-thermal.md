DJI has launched the **Matrice 5T**, a compact enterprise drone aimed at public safety, infrastructure inspection and surveying.

What stands out:

- **1K thermal:** a native **1280 × 1024** infrared sensor — four times the pixels of the common 640 × 512 format — with an ultra-high-resolution mode that outputs 2560 × 2048.
- **Low-light vision:** a wide camera with a large 1/0.98-inch sensor and f/1.8 aperture, plus AI enhancement for colour imagery in very dark scenes, and a telephoto camera with monochrome night vision.
- **Safety and autonomy:** LiDAR, mmWave radar and vision sensing for obstacle detection, onboard AI for target recognition, an IP55 rating and an operating range of −20 °C to 50 °C.
- **Endurance:** up to 59 minutes of flight time.

DRONELIFE notes that US availability is uncertain following the FCC's December 2025 decision to add foreign-produced drones and components to its Covered List.

**Why it matters:** higher-resolution thermal sensors are becoming the norm for search & rescue and inspection. For FPV and custom platforms, HD thermal cameras with digital video links bring similar capabilities to lighter, lower-cost airframes.

DRONELIFE señala que su disponibilidad en EE. UU. es incierta tras la decisión de la FCC de diciembre de 2025 de incluir drones y componentes fabricados en el extranjero en su «Covered List».

**Por qué importa:** los sensores térmicos de mayor resolución se están convirtiendo en el estándar en rescate e inspección. En plataformas FPV y a medida, las cámaras térmicas HD con enlace de vídeo digital acercan esas capacidades a drones más ligeros y económicos.

Sikorsky, part of Lockheed Martin, is continuing flight tests of its **Nomad** prototype under DARPA's **EVADE** (Early VTOL Aircraft Demonstration) programme. The initial flights took place in July.

- EVADE is a **$47 million** programme to demonstrate uncrewed VTOL aircraft of up to **150 kg** and fast-track their development.
- Among the objectives is flying for **12 hours** while carrying a **27 kg payload**.
- Nomad is a **flying-wing** design using a rotor-blown wing concept, where the two rotors push air over the wing to augment lift.
- Rather than a fixed end date, each design keeps flying until it meets the programme milestones.

**Why it matters:** VTOL aircraft with fixed-wing endurance are a fast-growing category for surveillance, inspection and logistics, and every one of them needs reliable long-range video and sensor payloads.
